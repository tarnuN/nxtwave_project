FAQs

Questions parents often ask

Can I ask about fees and scholarships?

Yes. Bharosa can share the information currently available, but ask a counsellor to confirm current fees, scholarships, and eligibility.

Can I visit a campus before deciding?

Ask Bharosa about arranging a visit. A counsellor can confirm the campus, available dates, and timings.

Can Bharosa confirm degree or placement details?

Bharosa only uses the information available to it. It will direct you to a counsellor when a detail needs current or official confirmation.
